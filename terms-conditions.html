Every garment is made to order based on your measurements, fabric and design choices agreed with our design team. Because each piece is handcrafted individually:

  • Production timelines will be confirmed at the time of order and depend on the complexity of embroidery and detailing.

  • Design changes requested after work has begun may affect the delivery timeline and final price.

3. Advance Payment & Pricing

An advance payment is typically required to confirm a custom order, with the balance due before delivery or at final fitting. Exact terms will be shared and agreed with you at the time of order.

4. Fittings & Alterations

We recommend attending all scheduled fittings so we can adjust the garment to your measurements. One round of minor alterations after the final fitting is generally included; extensive changes beyond the original design may be chargeable.

Because materials are cut and work begins soon after an order is confirmed, advance payments are generally non-refundable once production has started. Cancellation requests made before cutting/embroidery begins will be handled on a case-by-case basis.

Garments will be ready for collection or delivery once completed and fitted. Please inspect your garment at collection; any concerns should be raised promptly so we can address them.
