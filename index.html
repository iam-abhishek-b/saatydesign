The Atelier

Couture, the Way
It Was Meant to Be Made.

SAATY DESIGN is a boutique design studio — not a store, not a factory floor. Every garment that leaves our atelier has been sketched, fitted and finished for one person alone.

We work close to the skin: your measurements, your occasion, the story you want your outfit to carry. From a bridal lehenga worked entirely by hand to a single custom blouse, nothing here is repeated, resized or reordered from a rack.

From Sketch to Send-off

How a SAATY Garment
Comes to Be.

01

Sketch

We translate your references and occasion into an original design, sketched by hand.

02

Pattern Making

A paper pattern is drafted to your exact measurements — no standard sizing.

03

Fabric Selection

Silks, velvets and organzas are sourced and matched to the design.

04

Cutting

Each panel is cut by hand, fabric grain and motif placement considered piece by piece.

05

Hand Stitching

Our karigars construct the garment by hand, seam by seam.

06

Embroidery

Zardozi, Aari or Resham work is added, often taking weeks per piece.

07

Final Fitting

You return for a final fitting; we adjust until it sits perfectly.

08

Delivery

Pressed, packed and delivered — ready for the day it was made for.
