Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This is a general-purpose template provided as a starting point for the SAATY DESIGN website. It is not legal advice. Please have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer familiar with Indian data-protection law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) before publishing.

1. Who We Are

SAATY DESIGN ("we", "us", "our") is a boutique couture atelier based at 1st Floor, Above Vetic Clinic, 283, MIG, 1st Phase, 15th A Cross, Yelahanka New Town, Bengaluru 560064, India. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website, message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, or visit our studio for a consultation.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details you share with us, such as your name, phone number, email address and WhatsApp/Instagram handle.
  • Appointment and order details, including your occasion, measurements, fabric and design preferences, and fitting notes.
  • Photographs taken during fittings or of finished garments, where you have given consent — for example, for our portfolio or social media.
  • Website usage information such as pages visited and general device/browser information, collected automatically.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule consultations.
  • To design, fit and deliver your garment, including keeping records of measurements and preferences for future orders.
  • To communicate with you about your order via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To showcase our work in our portfolio or on social media, only with your consent.
  • To improve our website and services.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted parties who help us operate — for example, tailors, embroidery artisans and delivery partners involved in fulfilling your order — solely for that purpose.

5. WhatsApp & Instagram

When you contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram, your communication is also subject to the privacy policies of those platforms (Meta). We recommend reviewing their policies separately.

6. Data Retention

We retain client measurements and order history to make future orders and alterations easier, unless you ask us to delete them. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Your Choices

  • You may ask us what personal information we hold about you.
  • You may ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • You may withdraw consent for us to use your photographs in our portfolio at any time.

8. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us at hello@saatydesign.com or +91 91 87189 813.
